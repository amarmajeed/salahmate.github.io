Privacy Policy

SalahMate

SalahMate respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is used by the application and how it is handled.

1. Information We Use

Location
Your location is used to provide accurate Salat times, Iqamah times, and detect nearby mosques for automatic silent mode functionality.
Notifications
Notification permissions are used to deliver Adhan alerts, prayer reminders, and alarms.
Network Access
Internet access is used to retrieve accurate prayer times and related data from trusted APIs.

2. Personal Data Collection

SalahMate does not collect, transmit, or share personal information. Prayer settings, preferences, and location information remain stored locally on your device.

However, third-party services such as Google AdMob may collect data according to their own privacy policies when displaying advertisements.

3. Background Location

The app may use background location access to detect when you enter or leave a mosque. This enables automatic silent mode activation and restoration when leaving the mosque.

4. Battery Usage

Continuous location monitoring may increase battery usage. SalahMate is optimized to minimize battery consumption while maintaining reliable mosque detection and prayer functionality.

5. User Control

  • You can manually select your location instead of automatic detection.
  • You can disable location permissions at any time.
  • You can disable notifications at any time.
  • Your data is used solely for app functionality and is not shared by SalahMate.

6. Data Security

All app settings and locally stored information remain on your device. SalahMate does not maintain external servers containing your personal data.

7. Data Deletion Policy

SalahMate does not require account creation and does not store personal data on external servers.

Users can permanently delete all locally stored data at any time by:

  1. Uninstalling the SalahMate application, or
  2. Clearing the application's storage and data through device settings.

Once the application data is removed or the app is uninstalled, all locally stored information is permanently deleted from the device.
